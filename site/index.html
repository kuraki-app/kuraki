Right now, your whole life in photographs lives on someone else's computer — scanned, rented, one policy change from gone. Kuraki brings it home.

Why Kuraki

Own it. Find it. Keep it forever.

The comfort of a modern photo app, without handing your memories to a company that can lose them, mine them, or lock you out.

Own it

Your originals are written once and never touched again, filed under a plain originals/YYYY/MM/ tree you can open in any file browser. No database prison. Walk away with everything, anytime.

Find it

A fast, day-grouped timeline that stays smooth at tens of thousands of photos. Search by place, date, camera, or tag. Wander a map of where you've been. Rediscover today, years ago.

Keep it forever

Boring on purpose. Every upgrade snapshots your database first, duplicates are caught automatically, and verify re-checks every file for rot. Memories deserve boring.

The library

Everything a photo app should do — quietly.

A Google-Photos-style experience over a library that answers to no one but you.

Timeline

A smooth, endless scroll

Day- and month-grouped, virtualized so it stays fluid whether you have 500 photos or 500,000.

Viewer

Full-screen, keyboard-fast

An EXIF panel, in-browser video with seek, and one-click download of the untouched original.

Search

Find any moment

Filename, date range, media type, camera, place, and tags — full-text and instant. Save a search you run often.

Places

A map of where you've been

Geotagged photos clustered on a map, with reverse-geocoding done offline — no location ever leaves your server.

Albums · Memories

Gather & resurface

Hand-made albums, plus an "on this day" view that quietly brings back the moments you'd forgotten.

Tags · Ratings

Shape your library

Tag and rate photos, archive the clutter, and hide what you don't want in the timeline.

iOS & Android

Camera-roll backup, from your pocket

Pair the app to your server with a revocable token and every new photo backs itself up automatically — restart- and network-safe, resumable, and honest about anything that still needs attention. Browse, tag, and tidy your whole library on the phone, even offline.

Duplicates

Tidy, never destructive

A resumable, whole-library scan groups near-identical copies for you to resolve. Nothing is ever deleted on its own.

Get running

Home in three steps.

One process, one port. Point a domain at it when you're ready to reach it from anywhere.

STEP 01

Run the server

One container (ffmpeg and image decoding bundled) or a single static binary. Zero config to start.

STEP 02

Point your phone

Scan the pairing QR from the Devices page. Automatic camera-roll backup begins in the background.

STEP 03

Your memories, home

Import old libraries and Google Takeout too — capture dates, places, and captions all survive the move.

# bring it up on :3000 — API, media, and web UI from one origin
docker run -d -p 3000:3000 -v kuraki-data:/data ghcr.io/kuraki-app/kuraki
No lock-in

The exit is always open.

Lock-in is how the cloud keeps you. Kuraki is built to be left — because a home you can't leave isn't really yours.

  • Originals are byte-for-byte the files you imported — no re-encoding, no proprietary blobs.
  • The database stores pointers, never your photos. Delete Kuraki and your originals are still right there.
  • AGPL-3.0, open source. Read every line, run it forever, fork it if we ever disappear.
