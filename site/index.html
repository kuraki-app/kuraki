The three jobs of a library

Keep it. Find it. Own it.

Kuraki is built around what you actually do with a personal photo library over years — not a feature checklist.

Keep

Backup as a habit

Your camera roll backs itself up, and every original lands home intact.

  • Automatic phone backup (iOS & Android)
  • Write-once originals in a readable YYYY/MM layout
  • BLAKE3 dedup & scheduled backups
Find

Any moment, in seconds

One search language across the web timeline and the phone app.

  • Filter by date, place, camera, type, rating
  • Local OCR finds words inside screenshots
  • Places map with offline reverse geocoding
Own

Yours to leave with

Nothing about Kuraki traps your library inside it.

  • Originals never modified after import
  • Portable backup / restore & whole-library export
  • Open source, self-hosted, no account

In the library

Everything a photo app should do — quietly.

Timeline & viewer

Virtualized day/month grid that stays smooth on huge libraries, with an EXIF-rich lightbox.

Places

A map of your geotagged photos, with city and country resolved entirely offline.

Albums & favorites

Group by hand, star what matters, and revisit an "on this day" memories feed.

Duplicates

A perceptual hash groups near-identical copies to review — nothing is deleted for you.

Stacks

RAW+JPEG and Live/Motion pairs collapse to one primary, the rest a click away.

Real media pipeline

libvips + ffmpeg make HEIC/AVIF/RAW previews and video posters — with browser-safe derivatives.

Searchable text (OCR)

Opt-in local OCR reads text in screenshots and documents. Fully on-device.

Trash & integrity

30-day trash, and a scheduled re-checksum that proves every original is still intact.

From zero to backed up

Running in three steps.

STEP 01

Start the server

docker compose up -d on any box with Docker. libvips and ffmpeg come baked in.

STEP 02

Create your account

Open localhost:3000 and set an owner password. Zero config files to edit.

STEP 03

Pair your phone

Scan a QR code from the app; your camera roll backs up automatically, even in the background.

The kura promise

Built so you can leave.

A storehouse is only trustworthy if you can open it yourself. Kuraki is designed to hand your library back at any moment.

Plain, readable filesOriginals sit in originals/YYYY/MM/ — usable with or without Kuraki.
Portable backup & restoreOne SQLite-consistent archive; restore it on a clean machine and see recorded proof.
Whole-library exportDownload everything as a zip whenever you want. No permission needed.
Stays on your machineNo cloud calls — geocoding and OCR run locally. AGPL-3.0, auditable end to end.
